Enterprise HRIS

Multi-company Philippine payroll & HR — a complete, self-hosted system that runs on your own standard web hosting. No monthly SaaS fees.

Multi-company PH statutory payroll Employee self-service 16 roles · 30+ permissions Runs on shared hosting

GEEK Enterprise HRIS manages people, payroll, time, benefits, assets and HR workflows across multiple companies from a single installation. It ships as a ready-to-deploy package for standard cPanel shared hosting (PHP + MySQL) — you own the software and the data, with role-based access control and employee self-service built in.

What's inside

Full feature set

Payroll & Compliance

  • Multi-company payroll runs — prepare, compute, approve, lock
  • Philippine statutory: SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, withholding tax
  • 13th-month & bonuses — special-pay runs in bulk
  • Payslip PDF generation, per employee & period
  • Bank file export for salary crediting
  • Government contribution reporting

Employee Self-Service

  • Own profile, government IDs & bank on file
  • Generate & download own payslips and 13th-month
  • Leave balances & file leave requests
  • Overtime and loan / cash-advance requests
  • View benefits, beneficiaries & assigned assets
  • Self-service password change

Time, Leave & Attendance

  • Attendance import — CSV, biometric, or API
  • Configurable leave types & credits per company
  • Balances that auto-deduct on approval
  • Leave & overtime approval workflow (Dept Head / HR)
  • Daily logs: hours, late, overtime, status

Benefits & Assets

  • Record benefits (HMO / health, life, and custom types)
  • Beneficiaries with relationship & share
  • Company asset issuance (laptops, phones, etc.)
  • Return / reassign with remarks & full history
  • Bulk asset return on separation

HR & Talent

  • Recruitment — requisitions with placement, timeline, budget
  • Applications & interview tracking
  • Performance cycles & training records
  • Service tickets & requests
  • Onboarding / offboarding checklists

Projects & Finance

  • Projects with budgets by period
  • Loan & cash-advance ledgers with balances
  • Payroll-deductible obligations
  • Reports by staff, period, project, or organization

Administration & Security

  • Role-based access — 16 roles, 30+ granular permissions
  • Multi-organization scoping enforced on the backend
  • Create users, link logins to employee records
  • Bulk-provision employee logins (with CSV of credentials)
  • JWT sign-in, 15-minute idle logout, hashed passwords, audit trail

Branding & Dashboards

  • White-label — upload your own logo
  • Enterprise & per-organization dashboards
  • Org setup: departments & positions
  • Real-time storage monitoring
  • Works on desktop & mobile browsers

Technical

Specifications & requirements

BackendPHP 8 with PDO — no framework, no Composer packages required
DatabaseMySQL 8 (or MariaDB) via phpMyAdmin
Front-endPre-built single-page web app (static) — nothing to compile on the server
HostingStandard cPanel shared hosting (e.g. Bluehost, Zoom Hosting) — Apache/LiteSpeed
NeedsMySQL database, File Manager or FTP, SSL (free AutoSSL), optional SSH & cron
SecurityJWT authentication, bcrypt passwords, role/permission checks, idle auto-logout, audit log
FootprintLightweight — the whole app is a few MB; data grows with your records
OwnershipSelf-hosted — your server, your data, no per-seat SaaS fees
