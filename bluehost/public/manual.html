HRIS User Guide

One guide for everyone — signing in and self-service for staff, plus the HR, administrator, and superadmin tools on the GEEK Enterprise HRIS.

For: Staff · HR & Admin · Superadmin System: hris.exssi.com Jump to your role: use the contents below

The HRIS runs entirely in the browser and serves three kinds of people. Staff view their own records, payslips and file requests. HR & administrators manage people, payroll, leave, benefits, documents and the BIR forms. Superadmins look after the whole installation — email, backups, licensing, branding and the audit trail. Everyone signs in at the same address; what you can see is decided by your role, so you'll only ever be shown the parts that apply to you.

Getting started

Signing in for the first time

Your account is created for you by HR or your system administrator. You'll receive your email address and a temporary password to get in. You don't register or create your own account.

  1. Open the HRIS.

    In your web browser, go to https://hris.exssi.com. Works on a computer or phone.

  2. Enter your details.

    Type the Email and Password you were given, then click Sign in.

  3. You're in.

    Employees go straight to My Self-Service; if you belong to just one company you're taken right into it. That self-service menu on the left is your home base — everything about you lives there — and the 🔔 bell at the top-right shows your notifications.

Can't get in

Forgot your password

You can reset your own password without waiting for HR — as long as your account uses a working email address.

  1. Click "Forgot password?"

    On the sign-in page, click the Forgot password? link, enter your work email, and click Send reset link.

  2. Open the email.

    You'll get a message titled Reset your GEEK HRIS password. Click the link inside — it's valid for 1 hour and works only once.

  3. Set a new password.

    Type a new password (at least 8 characters) twice, then click Update password and sign in with it.

Didn't get the email?

For your privacy the page always says "if that email exists, we've sent a link" — so it never reveals who has an account. Check your spam folder. If it still doesn't arrive, your account's email may be missing or wrong; ask HR to check it, or to reset your password for you.

First thing to do

Set your own password

Please change the temporary password to something only you know, right after your first sign-in.

  1. Open My Self-Service.

    Click My Self-Service in the left menu.

  2. Go to the Security tab.

    Along the top of the page, click Security.

  3. Change it.

    Enter your current password, then a new password (at least 8 characters), and click Update password.

Keep it private

Never share your password. GEEK staff will never ask you for it. Use something you don't reuse on other websites.

Everyone

Sessions & lockout

Two automatic safeguards protect your account. They're normal — here's what to expect.

Automatic sign-out

After 3 minutes of inactivity (no clicking or typing) the system signs you out and shows a note on the login page — just sign in again. You can also sign out anytime with the Sign out link at the top right. On a shared computer, always sign out rather than only closing the tab.

Too many wrong passwords

If a wrong password is entered 5 times in a row for the same account, sign-in is locked for about 15 minutes as protection against guessing. Wait it out, or use Forgot password? to reset — a successful reset clears the lock.

Your records

The My Self-Service area

My Self-Service shows your own records only. It's organized into tabs across the top — here's what each one holds.

Profile

Your personal details, bank on file, government IDs (TIN, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG), and your employment record — employee number, company, engagement type, and rate.

Payslips

Every finalized payroll period, with the net pay and a button to generate and download your payslip as a PDF.

13th Month

Your own 13th-month pay and bonus amounts, once each run is finalized.

Leave

Your leave balances per type, a form to request leave, and the status of every request.

Attendance

Your recent daily logs — hours worked, late minutes, overtime, and the status for each day.

Overtime

File an overtime request (date and hours) and track its approval.

Loans

Request a loan or cash advance, and see your existing obligations with the remaining balance.

Benefits

Your benefits (such as HMO / health coverage) and the beneficiaries linked to each.

Assets

Company items issued to you — laptop, phone, etc. — including anything you've already returned.

Contributions

Your government contributions by period — SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and withholding tax.

Security

Change your password (do this on day one), and Connect Telegram to get approval alerts on your phone.

Something look wrong?

Your profile, bank details, government IDs, benefits, and assigned assets are maintained by HR — you view them here but can't edit them yourself. If anything is out of date or incorrect, contact HR and they'll update it for you.

Requests & approvals

Filing leave, overtime & cash advances

You can submit three kinds of requests yourself from My Self-Service. Each is sent for review and is approved — or rejected — by your Department Head or HR.

Request leave

Leave tab → pick a leave type and the dates, then submit. Your remaining balance per type is shown, and an approved leave is deducted from it automatically.

Request overtime

Overtime tab → enter the date and number of hours, then submit for approval.

Request a loan / advance

Loans tab → choose the type, amount, and reason, then submit. Once approved it appears with its balance.

Tracking status

After you submit, a request shows as Pending. It becomes Approved or Rejected once your Department Head or HR acts on it — check back on the same tab.

You'll be notified

You don't have to keep refreshing. When your request is decided you're notified by email, in the app's 🔔 bell (top-right), and on Telegram if you connected it (My Self-Service → Security → Connect Telegram). Approvers are alerted the same way the moment you submit, so nothing waits unseen.

Most-used task

Download your payslip

You can get your own payslip as a PDF the moment a payroll period is finalized — no need to request it from HR.

  1. Open the Payslips tab.

    Go to My Self-Service → Payslips. Each finalized period is listed with its net pay.

  2. Generate the PDF.

    On the period you want, click Generate PDF. The first time, the system builds your payslip document.

  3. View or save it.

    The button becomes View / Download PDF — click it to open the payslip in a new tab, where you can read, save, or print it.

Don't see a period yet?

Only finalized payroll periods appear here. If the period you're expecting is missing, payroll hasn't finalized it yet — check back later or ask HR.

Understanding the numbers

Reading your contributions

The Contributions tab lists, for each pay period, the mandatory Philippine deductions taken from your pay:

ItemWhat it is
SSSSocial Security System — your contribution toward sickness, maternity, retirement, and other benefits.
PhilHealthNational health insurance that helps cover hospital and medical costs.
Pag-IBIGThe Home Development Mutual Fund — savings that also let you access housing loans.
Withholding taxIncome tax withheld from your pay and remitted to the BIR on your behalf.

These same figures appear on each payslip, so you can always reconcile a period's deductions against this tab.

Good to know

Habits that keep things smooth

Keep your info current

Moved house, changed your number, or opened a new payroll bank account? Tell HR promptly — accurate details keep your pay and government filings correct.

Sign out on shared devices

If you're on a shared or public computer, click Sign out when you're done rather than just closing the tab. The 3-minute auto sign-out is a backstop, not a replacement.

Use a supported browser

A recent version of Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox works best. If a page looks off after an update, refresh with Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + R.

For HR & administrators

People & the 201 file

Under People (and Consultants) you keep the records for your organization. Access is role-based, so you only see the modules your administrator has granted. Open a person with Edit to update their personal details, government IDs, bank, and engagement.

Uploading documents (the 201 file)

Each employee has a 201 File section inside their Edit window for storing contracts, IDs, certificates and clearances.

  1. Open the person.

    People → Edit on the employee, then scroll to 201 File — Documents.

  2. Pick a category and file.

    Choose a category (Contract, Government ID, Certificate, …), optionally give it a title, choose the file, and click Upload.

  3. Manage later.

    Each document can be downloaded or deleted. Files are private — served only to signed-in users with document access, never public.

Upload limit

Each file must be 10 MB or smaller. Allowed types: PDF, images (PNG/JPG/…), Word, Excel, and text. Scan multi-page contracts to a single PDF and keep resolution reasonable to stay under the limit.

For HR & administrators

Leave credits & benefits

Give each employee their leave entitlements and benefits, and add your own types when the defaults don't fit your organization.

Leave credits

Leave & Attendance → set the credited days per leave type for an employee. Approved leave is deducted from the balance automatically.

Custom leave types

Add types beyond the defaults (Vacation, Sick, Emergency, Birthday, Bereavement, Maternity, Paternity) with their own credit amounts.

Benefits

Benefits → record HMO/health and other coverage per employee, with provider, policy number and coverage amount.

Beneficiaries

Attach beneficiaries to a benefit — name, relationship, % share and contact.

Only add what you use

Benefit and leave types are free-form. If your organization doesn't offer a particular benefit, simply don't add it — nothing is forced on every company.

For HR & administrators

Assets

Track company items (laptops, phones, etc.) from issue to return under Assets.

  • IssueAssign an item to an employee with its serial number, condition and issue date.
  • Return / reassignRecord a return or move an item to another person — with remarks and the condition on return.
  • On separationWhen someone resigns, record returned assets so there's a clear history for future reference.

Employees can see every item issued to them (and what they've returned) from My Self-Service → Assets.

For HR & administrators

Bulk employee import

Onboarding many people at once? Import them from a spreadsheet instead of typing each one.

  1. Download the template.

    On the People page click Template to get the CSV with the correct column headers.

  2. Fill it in.

    Add one row per person (name, email, employee number, engagement type, rate, dates, government IDs, …). Keep the header row unchanged.

  3. Import.

    Click Import CSV and choose your file. New people and their engagements are created; the result reports how many were added and flags any rows that need fixing.

For HR & administrators

Payroll & 13th month

Run payroll under Payroll: prepare a run for a period, compute it, review each person, then finalize. Finalized runs produce payslips staff can download themselves, and feed every downstream figure — reports, contributions, and the BIR forms.

  • 13th Month & BonusesProcess special-pay runs in bulk; amounts can be overridden per person before finalizing.
  • Loans & AdvancesIssue company loans/advances and approve employee requests; balances deduct through payroll.
  • Statutory deductionsSSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and BIR withholding are computed automatically using the current (2025) tables.
For HR & administrators

BIR forms

The BIR Forms page turns your posted payroll into print-ready working copies of the main withholding forms. Pick a form tab, choose the period (or payee), review and adjust the figures, then generate.

1601-C

Monthly compensation withholding. Pick a month, untick anyone who shouldn't be included, edit amounts, then generate the return with the monthly total.

0619-E

Monthly expanded withholding for consultants — the same editable worksheet, generating the monthly EWT total.

2307

Per-payee certificate for professional fees. Choose the consultant, the 5% or 10% rate, the months to certify, and the nature of payment.

2316

Annual compensation certificate per employee — all figures editable before you generate.

Each form opens in a new tab with a Print / Save as PDF button. The period totals to remit are also available in Reports as the BIR 1601-C and BIR EWT datasets.

Working copies, not the official PDF

These mirror the official form's fields for your review — they are not the official BIR PDF. File and pay the actual return through eBIRForms or eFPS using the figures shown.

For HR & administrators

Reports & export

Under Reports pick a dataset — employees, payroll (by period or project), statutory contributions, leave, attendance, benefits, assets, 13th month, or the BIR period totals — filter and sort it, then export to Excel (.xlsx), CSV, or a print-ready PDF.

For HR & administrators

Approvals & the daily digest

When staff file leave, overtime, or loan requests, approvers are alerted right away by email, the in-app 🔔 bell, and Telegram (if connected). A single approval by either the Department Head or HR decides a request, and the employee is notified of the outcome.

Daily digest

In Administration → Notifications, superadmins can switch on a daily digest — once a day each organization's approvers get one email/Telegram summary of everything still pending, only when there's something to act on. Set the time and toggle it on or off there.

For superadmins

Email delivery (SMTP)

Email powers password resets, approval alerts and the daily digest. Set it up once in Administration → Notifications → Email delivery.

  1. Choose SMTP.

    Select SMTP mailbox (recommended) over the server default for reliable delivery.

  2. Enter your mailbox.

    Host, port and encryption (e.g. hris.exssi.com · port 465 · SSL, or localhost · 587 · STARTTLS), the full email as username, the mailbox password, and the From address/name.

  3. Send a test.

    Use Send test to confirm it arrives, then Save.

The password stays on your server

The SMTP password is stored in a protected file on your own server and is never shown again in the app. Leave the password field blank when editing other settings to keep the saved one.

Delivery to Gmail / external providers

"Sent" means your server accepted the mail. For Gmail, Outlook and Yahoo to actually accept it, your domain needs SPF, DKIM and DMARC records (cPanel → Email Deliverability). Mail to your own domain works without them.

For superadmins

Telegram alerts

Give staff optional phone alerts through a free Telegram bot. In Administration → Notifications, create a bot with @BotFather, paste its token and connect — the webhook registers automatically. Staff then link themselves once via My Self-Service → Security → Connect Telegram. Everyone still gets email and the in-app bell regardless.

For superadmins

Branding

In Administration → Branding you can replace the GEEK logo and app name with your own. Best results with a transparent PNG, about 400 × 120 px (wide), up to 2 MB.

For superadmins

Backup & migration

Under Administration → Backup & Migration you can download a full backup ZIP (database + files) at any time, schedule automatic backups at a preferred time, and produce a migration package for moving to another host.

Scheduled backups

Turn on automatic backups and set the hour (e.g. midnight). This needs the one-time hourly cron job shown on the page added in cPanel — the app then decides when to run.

For superadmins

License

Administration → License shows your edition and user cap and lets you paste a license key to activate the installation. If a key is required and missing, an activation banner appears until a valid key is entered.

For superadmins & auditors

Audit trail

Administration → Audit Trail records every significant action — sign-ins, approvals, edits, uploads — with who did it, when, and from which IP. Anyone with the audit permission (e.g. the Auditor role) can read it.

  • FilterSearch by email or entity, and narrow by action, organization, and date range.
  • DetailsOpen an entry's details to see the before/after of a change.
  • Clear logsSuperadmins can clear the trail — everything, or only entries on/before a chosen date. The clear itself is recorded.
For superadmins

Statutory tables

Payroll uses the official Philippine 2025 tables: SSS 15% total (employee 5%, MSC ₱5,000–₱35,000), PhilHealth 5% (₱10,000–₱100,000), Pag-IBIG 2% (max ₱200), and the BIR TRAIN monthly withholding brackets. These are effective-dated, so past payroll keeps the rates that applied then. Always verify against the latest official circulars when they change.
