QUICK PATH (about 20-30 minutes)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Open  documentation/Installation-Guide.pdf  and follow it.
        - Create a subdomain (e.g. hris.yourcompany.com)
        - Create a MySQL database and user
        - Upload GEEK-HRIS-application.zip into the subdomain folder,
          then Extract it there
        - Import the database (db/schema.sql) and seed it
        - Copy app/settings.sample.php to app/settings.php and fill in
          your database details
        - Turn on free SSL (HTTPS)

  2. Sign in as the administrator and change the password.

  3. YOUR 14-DAY FREE TRIAL STARTS AUTOMATICALLY
        - No key needed to begin — the full system runs free for 14 days
          from first sign-in.
        - A banner shows how many trial days remain.
        - Try it on your own payroll. Your data stays yours.

  4. BEFORE THE TRIAL ENDS — ACTIVATE YOUR LICENSE
        - Go to  Administration -> License  (it shows your domain and
          the trial days left).
        - Click "Request key by email" (or email us yourself) to:

              geekhris@exssi.com

          Include: your domain, company name, edition
          (Starter / Business / Enterprise) and number of employees.
        - Once you've paid, we reply with your activation key. Paste it
          and click Activate — the app stays unlocked, data intact.

     NOTES:
        - If the trial ends before you activate, the app locks but NO
          data is lost — just enter a key to continue.
        - License keys are locked to your exact domain, so activate on
          the final domain you installed on.
