QUICK PATH (about 20-30 minutes)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Open  documentation/Installation-Guide.pdf  and follow it.
        - Create a subdomain (e.g. hris.yourcompany.com)
        - Create a MySQL database and user
        - Upload GEEK-HRIS-application.zip into the subdomain folder,
          then Extract it there
        - Import the database (db/schema.sql) and seed it
        - Copy app/settings.sample.php to app/settings.php and fill in
          your database details
        - Turn on free SSL (HTTPS)

  2. Sign in as the administrator and change the password.

  3. ACTIVATE YOUR LICENSE
        - Go to  Administration -> License
        - It shows your installation's domain
        - Click "Request key by email" (or email us yourself) to:

              geekhris@exssi.com

          Include: your domain, company name, edition
          (Starter / Business / Enterprise) and number of employees.
        - We reply with your activation key. Paste it and click
          Activate. The app is then fully unlocked.

     NOTE: License keys are locked to your exact domain, so request the
     key AFTER your site is live on its final domain.
